# Working notes

Dated notes written while the method and the studies were developed, from the first study of bivariate bicycle
circuits (early October 2026) to the audits of the dense Rank DP (21 October 2026). They are kept as a record of what
was tried and why; they are not maintained. Numbers in them can predate the final runs: the paper and the records in
`results/` are authoritative, with the audit in [../AUDIT.md](../AUDIT.md) and the benchmark in
[../BENCHMARK.md](../BENCHMARK.md).
